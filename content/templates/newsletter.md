# Template: Newsletter issue

**Topic:** {topic}
**Audience:** subscribers who want signal on AI, resilience, and high-agency
living. They are smart and time-constrained.
**Voice:** essayistic, candid, useful.
**Length:** 600–900 words.

Structure:
1. **Cold open** — a specific moment, observation, or data point. No
   throat-clearing.
2. **The argument** — three short sections, each headed with a sentence-case
   claim, not a question.
3. **The useful bit** — one tool, framework, or action the reader can apply
   this week.
4. **Sign-off** — one line. No PS-stacking.

Honesty rules:
- Hedge what you don't know.
- Never invent statistics.
- Link only to sources you'd defend.
