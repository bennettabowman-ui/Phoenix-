# Template: Product description / sales page

**Product:** {topic}
**Format:** digital download (PDF / Notion / prompt pack / toolkit).
**Voice:** direct, specific, no hype.
**Length:** ≤ 400 words.

Sections:
1. **One-line promise.** What it does for whom, in plain English.
2. **Who it's for / who it's not for.** Be willing to push wrong-fit buyers
   away — it builds trust.
3. **What's inside.** Bullet list of concrete artifacts. Counts, not adjectives.
4. **Why it works / who built it.** Brief, honest credibility.
5. **What you get & price.** Itemized.
6. **Refund policy.** Plain English. 30-day no-questions-asked is the default.

Forbidden: "transform", "unlock", "10x", "secret", emoji, fake scarcity.
