# Resilience Stack — launch thread (8 posts, ≤ 270 chars each)

---

**1/**
The most useful thing I built this year isn't an app or an essay. It's a
12-page worksheet I keep reprinting because every quarter it tells me a
different uncomfortable truth about what I actually depend on.

Today I'm releasing the system around it.

---

**2/**
It's called the Resilience Stack. Not a course. Not a manifesto. A modular
toolkit for thinking clearly when things get weird — for individuals and
households who want a real plan, not a bunker, not a vibe, not "AI will
sort it out."

---

**3/**
Two failure modes dominate this genre.

Doomer theater: bunkers, beans, dread as identity.

Sleek techno-optimism: "diversify your income," waved away with a
gesture.

Neither helps you on the day a platform bans you, your income halves, or
the grid wobbles for 96 hours.

---

**4/**
What helps is a system you've already run.

The Stack is eight modules: a capability audit, a dependency inventory,
five operational playbooks for the scenarios that actually hit knowledge
workers, a quarterly review ritual, a prompt library, and five printed
decision cards.

---

**5/**
Design rule for everything in it: it has to work on paper.

Notion is a convenience layer, not the core. The PDFs work without an
internet connection. The prompts work on any frontier model. The decision
cards live in your wallet.

That's the whole point — durable systems, not vendor lock-in.

---

**6/**
Who it's for: solo operators, freelancers, knowledge workers, household
captains, small co-ops. People who have looked at the last few years and
concluded the future is non-stationary and their decision quality matters
more than their plan.

Not for bunker theater. Not for hype. Not for fear.
